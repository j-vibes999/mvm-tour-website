## Research and preparation notes

- Four previously downloaded photo sources were reused without downloading them again. Their credited Commons/Flickr pages were checked against the source files.
- The Tad Saleuy photograph is titled for the road between Phonsavan and Sam Neua. Its sloping rock slabs, cascade and tree positions were visually matched to the destination photograph on [Discover Laos Today](https://discoverlaos.today/vi/houaphanh-province/thing-to-do/saleuy-waterfall). The original 1600 × 1200 image was downloaded from the photographer's [Flickr page](https://www.flickr.com/photos/princeroy/4308433259/).
- Tai Daeng uses a real photograph of a finished textile specifically documented as woven in Sam Tai, Houaphanh. Its original is 549 pixels wide; the available derivatives preserve that size limit.
- The only openly licensed festival photograph located was 450 × 299 pixels. It was used as a factual reference for an original illustration; the website asset is identified as AI-generated.
- Ban Na tower reference photographs by MJ Klein were inspected for the actual turquoise concrete frame, timber observation refuge, roof and access arrangement. Those photographs were used only as factual references for the labelled original illustration and are not served as website assets.
- The two final illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Prompts, page references and saved originals are recorded in `docs/image-prompts-north-central.json`. Both outputs were inspected for plausible scale, ordinary local materials and faithful subject identity.
- Verified on 2026-10-20: eight place mappings, six real photographs, two AI illustrations and twenty local WebP derivatives. Every source and derivative was checked with ffprobe; none of the derivatives exceeds its processed source width.
