## New program facts

- [Tourism Laos: Vientiane Capital](https://www.tourismlaos.org/central-provinces/vientiane-capital/) supports the Wat Si Saket, Patuxai, Pha That Luang and Buddha Park stops, plus the Mekong promenade and night market. The two-day arrangement is an editorial suggestion.
- [Official Tourism Luang Prabang: Misty Mountains — Nong Khiaw to Muang Ngoi](https://tourismluangprabang.org/recommended-trips/day-trips/misty-mountains-nong-khiaw-to-muang-ngoi/) supports Nong Khiaw's Nam Ou bridge and Pha Tok Cave, the boat approach to Muang Ngoi through Nong Khiaw, and walks toward Kaang Cave, rice fields and Ban Na. It supports keeping village and riverside downtime in these programs. Separate three-day stays are editorial suggestions.
- [Tourism Laos: Pha Daeng Peak](https://www.tourismlaos.org/luang-prabang-hiking-camping-at-the-best-viewpoint-in-laos-pha-daeng-peak/) supports Pha Daeng as an optional Nong Khiaw viewpoint walk, the need for suitable fitness and footwear, and adapting plans to trail conditions. A clear view or morning mist is not guaranteed.

Source pages contain transport schedules, fees and other operational details that can change. The programs do not reproduce those as current promises. Boat departures, road connections, opening hours, access, weather and activity suitability need checking when a real trip is arranged. Hotels, meals, transport, guides, tickets and entrance fees are not represented as included services.
